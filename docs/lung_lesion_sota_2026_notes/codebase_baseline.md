## 1. Architectures (fran)

- Factory `F/architectures/create_network.py:68-96` `create_model_from_conf`. Selectable `arch` strings: `UNet3D` (pytorch-3dunet derived, `F/architectures/unet3d/`), `nnUNet_v1` (`Generic_UNet_PL`, nnU-Net v1 generic UNet, 5 pools, 32 base features, InstanceNorm, LeakyReLU, `create_network.py:187-229`), `nnUNet` (dynamic-network-architectures PlainConvUNet, `F/architectures/nnunet.py:217-258`), `ResUNet` -> `create_resencunet_l_pl` (nnU-Net ResEnc-L layout, blocks 1,3,4,6,6,6, `nnunet.py:261-301`), `SwinUNETR` (MONAI defaults, ignores plan patch/feature size, `create_network.py:232-238`), `DynUNet`/`DynUNet_UB` (MONAI, `:150-182`). No MedNeXt, no Primus/ViT inside FRAN (ViT only in the external nnU-Net pilot, see section 9). `ResUNet` in the LITS-ROOST baseline is described as "residual, self-attention" (`/home/ub/code/fran/fran/run/nnfoundation/SPEC_liver_lungs.md` Baselines table), while `nnunet.py` builds plain ResEnc-L; the two were not reconciled in the files read.
- Default in the workbook: `E` sheet `model_params`, `arch` manual_value = `ResUNet` (ResEnc-L layout); `optimizer` Adam; `deep_supervision` 1; `lr` 0.01; `compiled` 0. The nodes baselines in the nnFoundation spec used `nnUNet_v1` (`SPEC.md` Baselines row, `/home/ub/code/fran/fran/run/nnfoundation/SPEC.md`).
- Topology derived from spacing and patch: `nnunet.py:185-214` `_compute_topology` calls nnU-Net v2 `get_pool_and_conv_props(spacing, patch, 4, 999999)`; features 32*2^i capped at 320 (3D). ResEnc-L: `conv_bias=True`, InstanceNorm eps 1e-5, LeakyReLU (`nnunet.py:274-292`). Decoder 1 block per stage.
- det3d architectures: MONAI `RetinaNet` with ResNet-50-style bottleneck backbone (layers 3,4,6,3), FPN `returned_layers [1,2]` (`D/architectures/create_detector.py:60-95`; not pretrained, `pretrained_backbone=False`); `retinaunet` = nnDetection-style plain-conv encoder (5 stages, 32->320, strides 1,2,2,2,2, `D/detection/retinaunet_network.py:8-9,36-60,81-107`) + UFPN decoder (`D/detection/arch/nndet/decoder/base.py`) + class/box heads (`D/detection/retinaunet.py:41-150`) + a segmentation head; the nnDetection `RetinaUNetV001` path adds BCE classifier and GIoU regressor (`/home/ub/code/nnDetection/nndet/ptmodule/retinaunet/v001.py:20-36`). Spreadsheet default `arch` for det is `retinanet` (`ED` `model_params`); production lung model LIDCA-GYRO is `retinaunet` (`SPEC_liver_lungs.md` Baselines; `STEP2_RETINAUNET_PLAN.md` target spec).

## 2. Preprocessing (fran)

- Orientation to RAS, then MONAI `Spacingd` (trilinear image, nearest label), no separate-z handling for anisotropy: `F/preprocessing/fixed_spacing.py:139-160` (SpI/SpL). Spacing per plan row, hard-coded in the workbook: lung plans `0.8,0.8,1.5` (x,y,z) (`E` `plans` rows lungs 1-3); det lung plans 1-2 use `[0.703125,0.703125,1.25]`, 3-4 use `0.8,0.8,1.5` (`ED` `plans_det`). SPEC states "FRAN's [anisotropy] is mostly hardcoded" (`SPEC.md` Overview item 3, Anisotropy table).
- Patch tensor shape `[d0, d0, d1]` from `patch_dim0/patch_dim1` (`F/configs/helpers.py:97-100`); lungs plans: 128x128x96, 192x192x96, 128x128x64 (`E` `plans` lungs 1-3); det: 192x192x80 train, 128x128x64 val (`ED`).
- Intensity: clip to dataset foreground percentile range [0.5, 99.5] (`F/preprocessing/globalproperties.py:56,268-327`; `datasetanalyzers.py:208`) then global z-score with dataset (clipped) mean/std (`NormaliseClipd`, `fixed_spacing.py:166-171`, `set_normalization_values` `:205-211`; applied again in the dataloader from `mean_fg`/`std_fg`, `F/managers/data/main.py:1028-1033`). Not a fixed CT HU window. det3d plans carry `intensity_a_min -1024 / a_max 300` for scaling (`ED` `plans_det`, `D/preprocessing/object_bounded.py:15` imports `ScaleIntensityRanged`). Per-image z-score exists only as the nnFoundation-parity ablation (`SPEC.md` F-10).
- Storage: resampled tensors stored as `.pt` then packed into HDF5 shards; foreground/background flat voxel indices precomputed per case (`F/preprocessing/hdf5_shards.py`, `F/transforms/fg_indices.py:1-105`; optional background subsampling every 5th index `fg_indices.py:84-144`, `fixed_spacing_fgonly.py:23-26`). Half-precision storage option (`fixed_spacing.py:103`).
- Cropping modes in plan `mode`: `source` (whole resampled image), `lbd` (label-bounded: crop to bounding box of a localiser/region label, margin `expand_by` mm), `rbd` (region-bounded), `pbd`, `whole` (resize whole image to patch). `F/preprocessing/labelbounded.py:51-130,193-262`. Lung plans use `lbd` with `expand_by 0` (`E` `plans` lungs 1-3).
- Cascade/localiser: lung production detector is "lbd after TOTALSEG-NJUGU" i.e. a TotalSegmentator-style multi-organ FRAN model produces a region mask (lungs) first (`SPEC_liver_lungs.md` Baselines table; `E` `plans` rows with `imported_folder .../predictions/totalseg/LITS-1439` and `TSL.label_region` remapping). Inference: `F/inference/cascade.py` `CascadeInferer`: `WholeImageInferer` (image resized to patch size, `cascade.py:76-135`), bbox from selected localiser labels expanded by 10 (`F/inference/helpers.py:289-304`), patch inference inside the box.
- LIDC labels: 1 = lung, 2-6 = nodule category (benign ... malignant, `/s/fran_storage/conf/best_runs.yaml` lung block). Lung plan remaps `{1:1,2:2,3:2,4:3,5:4,6:4}` or `{1:0,2:1,3:1,4:2,5:3,6:3}` (`E` `plans` lungs 1-3); `fg_indices_exclude=1` removes the lung label from foreground sampling (`E` lungs `fg_indices_exclude`).
- Detection preprocessing: boxes from connected components of the label map (one box per component), dusting threshold optional (`dusting_mm`, `ignore_labels_cc=1`), sidecar CSV with boxes (`D/preprocessing/object_bounded.py:10-60`, `F/preprocessing/helpers.py:27-31`, `F/preprocessing/hdf5_shards_det.py`).

## 3. Patch sampling (fran)

- Training loader keys for LBD: `"Ld,Rtr,L2,E,F1,F2,Affine,ResizePC,N,IntensityTfms"` (`F/managers/data/main.py:1461`). `Rtr` = `RandCropByFlatIndicesd` (`main.py:283-358`): per sample choose fg with probability pos/(pos+neg), centre drawn uniformly from the stored fg (or bg) voxel indices; crop read directly from the HDF5 shard (`LoadHDF5Cropd`, `main.py:472-510`). Parameters: `pos = fgbg_ratio` (manual_value 3 in `E` `dataset_params`, i.e. 75% of samples centred on foreground), `neg = 1` (`main.py:1117-1123`); `samples_per_file` 2 (`E` lungs); `batch_size` 5; `cache_rate` 0 (`E` `dataset_params`). A `prezoom_scale` multiplies the crop (`main.py:1115-1116`; manual 1).
- Foreground = any non-excluded label (all nodule categories pooled for sampling); no per-class, per-lesion-size or hard-negative aware sampling; no stored lesion list used for sampling (blob/instance info exists only in det sidecars). There is an LBD "extended bbox" centre sampler (`RandCropExtendedBBoxd`, `main.py:421-470`, `F/transforms/crop_indices.py`) that draws centres from boxes around lesions with sizes (128,160,192)x(64,96,128).
- Validation: grid (sliding) patches stitched per case (`F/managers/data/valid_patch_stream.py`, `F/evaluation/patch_stream_loss.py`, `F/managers/unet.py:166-262`); `val_sampling` fraction option.
- det3d: balanced sampler positive fraction 0.3 (`ED` `loss_params`), `fgbg_ratio` shared with fran; `samples_per_file` 1 (`ED` `plans_det`); object-bounded patch generation (`D/preprocessing/object_bounded.py`).

## 4. Augmentation (fran, CPU workers)

- Spatial: RandFlip on spatial axes 0 and 1 only (not z) with prob `flip.prob` (`F/managers/data/main.py:1037-1046`); RandAffine, p 0.3, rotate range [0.20, 0.20, 0.15] rad, scale +/-0.15, no translation, no shear (`E` `affine3d`; `main.py:1063-1068`). Crop is pad/crop to patch (`ResizeWithPadOrCropd`, `main.py:1076-1080`).
- Intensity (`main.py:1047-1062`): RandScaleIntensity, RandRandGaussianNoise (std limits [0.05,0.18]), RandShiftIntensity, RandAdjustContrast (gamma [0.7,1.3]), each probability 0.1 (`E` `transform_factors`). No blur, no low-resolution simulation, no gamma-inversion, no elastic, no mixup/copy-paste.
- GPU option: `DataManagerBTfms` (batched GPU flips/affine/resize, `F/managers/data/batch_tfms.py:20-70`, trainer flag `batch_tfms`); `Dev` ToDevice transform exists (`main.py:1025`). Default path is CPU.
- det3d: box-synchronised RandFlip + RandAffine + ResizeWithPadOrCrop + `ClipBoxToImage` (`D/transforms/gpu_det.py:45-190,355-409`), run on GPU; plus the same intensity transforms.

## 5. Loss, optimiser, schedule, precision (fran)

- Loss: MONAI Dice (multi-class, softmax, background excluded) + cross-entropy, weights 0.5/0.5 (`F/evaluation/losses.py:92-141,175-205`; `E` `loss_params` has `lambda_ce 1, lambda_dice 1`, but `DeepSupervisionLoss` constructs the inner loss with its defaults (`losses.py:240-244`)). Masked Dice used for stitched validation. Deep supervision: nnU-Net weights 1/2^i, last level dropped and renormalised (`losses.py:262-275`), scales from pool kernels (`F/managers/unet.py:436-456`). No focal, Tversky, boundary, lesion-wise or instance-aware loss. Blob loss is analysed in `/home/ub/code/fran/specs/blob_loss_notes.md` only; no implementation in the package.
- Optimiser: Adam (default) / AdamW / SGD selectable (`F/managers/unet.py:305-326`); weight decay 0 by default; lr 0.01 (`E` `model_params`). Scheduler: ReduceLROnPlateau, patience 30 (monitor `train0_loss`), checked every 2 epochs (`unet.py:328-344`; `E` `model_params` scheduler_*). No warm-up, no poly decay (poly/warm-up only planned in nnFoundation S2-C, `SPEC.md` F-10).
- Epochs: `epochs=600` default, validation every 2 epochs, early stopping patience 30 epochs-equivalent, `bf16-mixed` (`F/trainers/trainer.py:223-246,302-306`). Observed run lengths 199-439 epochs (best-runs tables in `SPEC.md`, `SPEC_liver_lungs.md`). No EMA anywhere in `F/` or `D/` (grep `ema` found nothing relevant); SWA callback optional in det3d (`D/trainers/trainerdet.py:403-408`, `use_swa` plan key).
- det3d optimiser: SGD momentum 0.9, nesterov flag, weight decay 3e-5 in sheet (`ED` `nndet`), implemented in `D/managers/det_schedule.py:17-35` with ReduceLROnPlateau (patience 30); STEP2 doc records GYRO as Adam lr 0.01 (`STEP2_RETINAUNET_PLAN.md`), so the checkpointed config and the current sheet differ. bf16-mixed, gradient clip 1.0 for retinaunet (`D/trainers/trainerdet.py:212,225-226`). Default `epochs` 600; plan `max_epochs` 300.

## 7. Inference (fran)

- Sliding window: MONAI `SlidingWindowInferer`, ROI = training patch, default overlap 0.0 and mode `constant` in `BaseInferer` (`F/inference/base.py:150-158,228-236`); `PatchInferer` default overlap 0.25 with `grid_mode="gaussian"` (`F/inference/cascade.py:149-153`); entry points use 0.2 (`base.py:559`) or 0.5 (`base.py:610`). `safe_mode` stitches on CPU with batch 1.
- No test-time mirroring in fran (grep `tta|flip` finds none in `F/inference/{base,cascade,ensemble}.py`). det3d has a placeholder `mirror_tta_forward` (`D/inference/mirror_tta.py:6-15`, flips on last two dims, averages logits, not wired) and passes `num_tta` (default 0) to the nnDetection predictor (`D/inference/retinaunet_nifti.py:118-150`).
- Ensembling: `EnsembleInferer` (`F/inference/ensemble.py:108-340,623-676`): members run as base or cascade, outputs fused by mean logits when multi-channel, else MONAI `VoteEnsembled`.
- Post-processing: k-largest connected components per organ (`k_largest`, lungs `k_largest: 2` in `best_runs.yaml`, applied via `KeepLargestConnectedComponent`, `base.py:263,442`); `MergeTouchingLabelsd` (`base.py:265-273`, label_analysis plan); resize to original grid. No lesion size filter / probability-threshold calibration in the standard path (dust filtering used in the scorer, 3 mm).
- det3d inference: sliding-window with `val_patch_size` [512,512,208] overlap 0.25 constant (`D/architectures/create_detector.py:19-20,107-114`); boxes: score threshold 0.02, topk 1000 per level, NMS 0.22, 25 detections/image (`ED` `postproc_params`; `create_detector.py:98-105`). nnDetection path: model-level weighted NMS IoU 0.1 (`batched_weighted_nms_model`) plus tile-position weighting (`BoxEnsemblerSelective`) (`D/inference/patch.py:140-215`). Cascade: region box from FRAN localiser, patch RetinaUNet inside, inverse transform of boxes to world coordinates (`D/inference/cascade.py`, `post.py:151-250`). No WBF across models/folds; no model ensembling in det3d (one checkpoint).

## 10. Conspicuously simple or absent (observation only)

- No pretrained encoder inside FRAN or det3d at survey time; foundation weights live only in the separate nnU-Net venv; det3d hook not present.
- Arch choices are CNN only (plain/ResEnc UNet, DynUNet, a default-config SwinUNETR); no MedNeXt, no transformer-encoder UNet trained in FRAN.
- Spacing hard-coded in the workbook (0.8x0.8x1.5 or 0.703x0.703x1.25), trilinear resampling, no per-dataset spacing planning, no separate-z resampling; anisotropic 1.25-1.5 mm z fixed for lungs.
- Clip-percentile + global z-score, no per-image or HU-window options other than the nnFoundation pilot.
- Foreground oversampling is a fixed 3:1 voxel-index rule; no lesion-size-aware, class-aware, instance-aware or hard-negative-mining sampling in segmentation; det3d uses a hard-negative anchor sampler only.
- Loss is Dice+CE with deep supervision; no focal/Tversky/blob/instance-aware loss implemented; no calibration or lesion-level objective.
- Lung segmentation model (LIDC-JOT/EDGES) trains on LIDC consensus masks pooled over annotators; no annotator-uncertainty handling; nodule category labels remapped but not used as a loss.
- Augmentation light (all intensity ops p=0.1, flip only x/y, no elastic, blur, simulate-low-res, no synthetic-lesion or copy-paste insertion).
- No EMA; plateau LR schedule rather than poly/cosine; single fold per model; no ensemble over folds in production; no test-time mirroring in practice.
- No synthetic or pseudo-labelled data, no semi-supervised use of unlabelled CT; only LIDC for lungs.
- Detector: no WBF across models, no false-positive-reduction second stage (a ResNet3D classifier on boxes exists as a scaffold, `F/preprocessing/lesion_patches.py`, `plans_dot` sheet, not part of the lung pipeline), no FROC logged during training.
- Evaluation: one evaluator for FROC lives in a separate repo; no LUNA16-protocol comparison; no per-lesion-size breakdown for lungs; no external lung test set scored.
